THE NUMBERS BEHIND THE MAP

A cost signal, not a crystal ball.

The estimated minimum shows what regular gas could cost using regional wholesale prices. It helps you spot a gap between the pump and the cost of fuel. It does not predict when a station will change its price.

Where the prices come from

How the estimate is calculated

(Wholesale + 51.3¢ tax & fees + delivery) × 1.0918

We reuse Dane Fuel’s automatic terminal-based scenario. Federal gas tax is 18.4¢, Wisconsin tax is 30.9¢, and the state inspection fee is 2¢ per gallon. The estimate uses the 9.18% terminal markup and rounds upward to a tenth of a cent.

Each county uses its geographically nearest quoted market center, including adjacent states. This is a regional proxy; actual terminal location and retailer delivery costs may differ. Six southeast counties use regular reformulated gasoline (87RE10), rather than conventional ULC10.

Wisconsin’s complete cost calculation compares terminal and invoice/replacement branches. Retailer costs, eligible prior-day prices and exceptions are not public here. This estimate is neither a verified legal minimum nor a guarantee of a future pump price.

Reading “room to fall”

We compare the lowest fresh, eligible price collected in a county with its estimated floor. A gap of at least 15¢ is labeled “Room to fall”; a gap of 8¢ or less is “Near estimated floor”; between those is “Keep an eye on prices.” These are transparent comparison thresholds, not a trained forecast. Limited brand coverage can miss cheaper stations. Savings are hypothetical if the price reaches the floor; no arrival date is estimated.

GasBuddy reports expire after 24 hours. Direct quotes expire 24 hours after retrieval. Published snapshots expire 24 hours after the source update. Individual price-change times are unavailable for either source. Terminal estimates are flagged after 96 hours. Missing reports never become zero prices.
